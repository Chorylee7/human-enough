# Human Enough｜人

[![License: CC BY-SA 4.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-CC_BY--SA_4.0-lightgrey.svg)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/deed.zh-hans)

**你接收到了很多信息，但你不欠每一条信息一个回应。**

**You take in a lot of information. You don't owe every signal a response.**

一份写给容易注意到细节、习惯承担责任、反复解释自己的人的生活手册。

它讨论：没回的消息、别人的情绪、拒绝后的愧疚、停不下来的复盘，以及那些让休息也变成考试的自我要求。

A handbook for people who notice the details, take on responsibility, and keep explaining themselves. It explores unanswered messages, other people's feelings, guilt after saying no, endless mental replays, and the expectations that turn even rest into a test.

## 阅读 / Read

### [简体中文：《人》](./人.md) · [English: Human Enough](./human.en.md)

每章从一个具体场景开始，留下一条可以采取的行动，以及一个可以结束处理的位置。

Each chapter starts with an everyday scene, offers a possible action, and gives you a place to stop processing it.

## 你会读到 / Chapters

- 你看见了，不代表你要负责。
- 没有人给你打分的时候，你也在交卷。
- 你可以让别人失望，而不必成为一个坏人。
- 别人皱了一下眉，你已经修改了整个人生。
- 你不需要证明自己没有恶意。
- 不是所有不舒服，都需要立刻消失。
- 有些问题，想得更久也不会更清楚。
- 你小时候很懂事，长大后可以重新学不会。
- 你已经很累了，不需要再成为一个更好的人。
- 有些环境，确实需要离开。

- Noticing it doesn't make it your responsibility.
- No one is grading you, but you're still handing in the assignment.
- You can disappoint someone without being a bad person.
- Someone frowns, and you rewrite your entire life.
- You don't have to keep proving you meant well.
- Not every uncomfortable feeling needs to disappear immediately.
- Some questions won't become clearer with more thinking.
- You were the good kid; now you can learn to be a beginner.
- You're already tired; you don't have to become a better person today.
- Some environments really do need to be left.

## 关于这份文档 / About

当前为第一版，欢迎讨论和修订。这里的“高敏感”是生活体验的称呼，不是诊断；不需要先完成一个人格测试才能阅读。

正文区分生活整理与参考依据，不把“早熟的人一般都晚熟”当作心理学定律，也不把现实中的伤害归咎于个人敏感。专业支持的边界与参考资料见正文末尾。

This is the first edition, open to discussion and revision. “High sensitivity” describes a lived experience here, not a diagnosis. No personality test is required. The text distinguishes everyday observations from reference material, treats sayings about early and late maturity as reflections rather than psychological laws, and does not blame real harm on someone's sensitivity. References and the limits of self-help appear at the end of each edition.
