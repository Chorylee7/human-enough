# 封面设计记录

生成方式：OpenAI 内置 imagegen。

概念：接收很多信息，但不需要回应所有信息。以温暖的纸张、透明的人形和细线表现开放与选择，大部分信号经过，少数橙色信号留下。

生成提示词：

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide GitHub README cover for a Chinese open-source reflective life handbook titled 人.md. Create an exquisite abstract editorial book-cover artwork, landscape 2:1. Concept: receiving many signals without needing to respond to all of them. Warm off-white tactile paper background. A small gently curved open human-like silhouette made of translucent frosted paper, surrounded by many fine graphite threads and floating specks approaching from the outer edges; most threads gently bend away and drift past, only a few warm orange threads rest inside the figure. The figure is permeable and calm, not armored or isolated. Subtle handmade printmaking textures, restrained contemporary art direction, airy negative space, poetic, sophisticated, quiet yet memorable. Integrate very large beautifully typeset black title text exactly '人.md' in the open left portion, with the abstract figure on the right. No additional text. Avoid brain illustrations, medical iconography, stock wellness graphics, neon gradients, lotus flowers, clutter, watermark. Make the text sharply legible and the composition meaningful at small GitHub display sizes.
